Check these before you buy

  • Condition: cosmetic grade does not tell you battery health or repair history. Read the actual listing.
  • Battery and locks: ask for battery health and ensure phones or laptops are free of activation locks and organisational management.
  • Warranty and returns: confirm who provides support, the coverage and return postage terms.
  • Compatibility: confirm Australian network support, keyboard layout, charger, console region and included accessories.
  • Final price: compare shipping, fees and the price of the same product new. A used price is not automatically a saving.
