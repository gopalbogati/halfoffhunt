How it works

{{brand}} runs scheduled scans of configured Australian stores approximately every 30 minutes; scheduling delays and unavailable feeds can reduce coverage. An item only makes the list if it is in stock, priced in Australian dollars, and at least 50% below the store's own "was" price.

What the badges mean

  • -62%: the discount against the store's listed original price.
  • NEW: first seen in the last 6 hours.
  • Possible pricing error: 90% off or more on an item that was $100 or more. These can be real clearance, or a mistake the store may cancel.

Checking a deal

Prices change quickly and stock sells out. The price at the store's checkout is the one that counts. A "was" price is the retailer's claim; if a discount looks too good to be true, compare it on another store first.

How we make money (disclosure)

Some links on this site, in our alerts and in our feed are affiliate links. If you buy through one, the retailer may pay us a small commission. You pay the same price. Affiliate status never decides whether a deal is listed or where it ranks: the list is sorted purely by discount, price and time, the same way for every store.
