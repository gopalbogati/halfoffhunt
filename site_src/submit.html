Share a deal or list your store

Independent retailers and shoppers can suggest Australian offers for review. Submissions are not published automatically, and submitting does not guarantee a listing.

Submit through GitHub

A free GitHub account is required. Submissions and replies are public: do not include passwords, private customer data, affiliate credentials or personal contact details.

Submit a deal or store

What to include

  • Retailer name and direct product or sale URL.
  • Current AUD price, comparison price and the source of that comparison.
  • Expiry date, coupon code, shipping costs, stock and location restrictions.
  • Whether you own the store or have an affiliate relationship.
  • For ongoing listings: a permitted product feed with prices, stock, currency and product links.

Report an issue or request removal

Report an expired offer, incorrect price or removal request. Include the product URL and explain the correction needed. No response time is guaranteed.
