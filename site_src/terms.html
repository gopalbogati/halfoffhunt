Using Half Off Hunt

Half Off Hunt is a free discovery index. Purchases, payments, delivery, returns and warranties are handled by the retailer, under their terms.

Check before buying

Listings are snapshots of retailer data. We cannot guarantee availability, discount accuracy, delivery costs or fulfilment. A retailer’s comparison price is not proof of a historical lowest price. Confirm the selected size, colour, seller, final delivered price and return policy at checkout.

Coverage and ranking

We scan a limited catalogue from configured stores; we do not cover every Australian store or every product. “Top picks” rotates stores using advertised discounts, and is not a quality recommendation. Very large discounts are not proof of a pricing mistake.

Links and submissions

Affiliate links may earn commission. Public submissions require review. Do not submit private data or content you do not have permission to share. Report incorrect listings or request removal.
